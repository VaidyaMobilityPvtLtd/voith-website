With **Huaxin Cement Narayani**, VOITH made one of its most significant diversification
moves — entering Nepal's construction-materials sector with a world-class cement
operation. Production began in **May 2022**, backed by a **USD 250 million** investment.

## Scale that builds a nation

Strategically located between **Kathmandu and Pokhara**, the plant was engineered for
both capacity and reach. Its headline figures tell the story of an industrial undertaking
built for the long term:

- **USD 250M** total investment
- **4,000 tons per day** production capacity
- **~1,000 personnel** employed across operations
- A central location serving Nepal's busiest construction corridor

## A partnership of expertise

The venture pairs VOITH's deep understanding of the Nepali market with the global
manufacturing know-how of **Huaxin Cement Co. Ltd.** — one of the world's established
cement producers. Together, the partnership brings modern, efficient, and consistent
cement production to a market with rising infrastructure demand.

> Cement is the foundation of national development. By producing it at scale and to
> international standards, we are helping build Nepal — literally.

## Beyond the plant

The impact of Huaxin Cement Narayani extends well past its production lines:

1. **Employment** — around a thousand direct jobs, plus a wider supplier ecosystem
2. **Import substitution** — strengthening domestic supply and reducing reliance on imports
3. **Quality** — bringing globally benchmarked materials to Nepali builders and projects
