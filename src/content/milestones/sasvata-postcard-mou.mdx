VOITH's hospitality venture, **Sasvata Wildlife Resort**, has signed a strategic
partnership with **The Postcard Hotel**, one of Asia's most acclaimed luxury boutique
hospitality brands, marking a significant milestone for Nepal's high-value tourism
sector.

## A shared vision for Nepal's tourism

The partnership reflects a shared vision to position Nepal not as a destination for mass
tourism, but as a global leader in boutique, experiential, and nature-based travel.
Located on the doorstep of the UNESCO-listed Chitwan National Park, Sasvata Wildlife
Resort aims to showcase Nepal's extraordinary wildlife, culture, and biodiversity through
personalized, world-class hospitality experiences.

> Positioning Nepal not as a destination for mass tourism, but as a global leader in
> boutique, experiential, and nature-based travel.

## Nepalese investment, international expertise

The collaboration is being led by **Ms. Savanna Vaidya Basnyat**, representing VOITH's
Hospitality Wing, and brings together Nepalese investment and international hospitality
expertise through a **Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)** partnership model focused on
quality tourism, conservation, and long-term destination development.

## A bigger ambition for South Asia

Beyond Nepal, Sasvata and The Postcard Hotel share a broader ambition: to help position
South Asia as one of the world's premier wildlife destinations, highlighting the region's
remarkable biodiversity, conservation success stories, and authentic nature-based
experiences. Through this partnership, they aim to contribute to a new tourism narrative
centered on quality, sustainability, and meaningful engagement with nature.

## About The Postcard Hotel

With a growing portfolio of award-winning properties across South Asia, The Postcard Hotel
has earned a reputation for redefining luxury through intimate, authentic, and
destination-driven experiences. Together, Sasvata and The Postcard seek to establish a new
benchmark for hospitality in Nepal while supporting a broader vision of South Asia as a
globally recognized destination for wildlife and conservation tourism.

This MOU laid the foundation for **The Postcard on the Rapti River by Sasvata Wildlife
Resort**, scheduled to open on 13 October 2026 in Meghauli, Chitwan.
