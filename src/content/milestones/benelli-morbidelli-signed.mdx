Two of Italy's most storied motorcycle marques, **Benelli** and **Morbidelli**, have
officially joined VOITH's growing two-wheeler division in Nepal. The agreements,
signed in December 2025, mark a decisive step in VOITH's ambition to move beyond
distribution and toward **building motorcycles in Nepal**.

## A century of Italian heritage

Founded in Pesaro in 1911, Benelli is one of the oldest motorcycle manufacturers in
the world, celebrated for its inline-four engines and modern naked and scrambler
platforms. Morbidelli, a legendary name in Grand Prix racing, returns to the road with
a contemporary line-up that pairs racing pedigree with everyday usability.

Bringing both brands under one roof gives VOITH a complementary portfolio:

- **Benelli:** modern roadsters, scramblers, and adventure-styled motorcycles
- **Morbidelli:** heritage-inspired models with a distinctive design language
- A shared service, parts, and after-sales backbone across VOITH's network

## More than importing, building locally

The signing is not the finish line; it is the foundation for **local SKD assembly**.
By assembling motorcycles in Nepal, VOITH aims to develop domestic technical expertise,
shorten supply chains, and create skilled manufacturing jobs.

> We are not just selling motorcycles. We want to build them here, with Nepali hands
> and Nepali talent.

This approach mirrors VOITH's broader philosophy of *Serving · Caring · Growing
Together*, investing in capability, not just commerce.

## What it means for riders

For Nepali riders, the partnership translates into:

1. Access to authentic Italian engineering, sold and serviced locally
2. Faster parts availability and warranty support through VOITH's dealer network
3. A pathway to locally assembled models that are better suited to Nepal's roads and price points
