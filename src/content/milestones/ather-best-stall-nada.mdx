At the **NADA Auto Show** — Nepal's premier automotive exhibition — Vaidya Energy's
**Ather** electric scooters were awarded **Best Stall**, a recognition that reflects both
the brand's design-led presence and its growing leadership in Nepal's EV market.

## Nepal's No. 1 electric scooter brand

Ather, distributed in Nepal by **Vaidya Energy** under the VOITH group, has rapidly
become the country's most recognised electric two-wheeler. The Best Stall award at NADA
is more than a showcase accolade — it signals consumer confidence in intelligent,
connected, and genuinely usable electric mobility.

The stall drew crowds with:

- Live demonstrations of Ather's connected dashboard and ride modes
- Hands-on experience of fast-charging and range capabilities
- Conversations with the team behind Nepal's expanding EV charging network

## Why it matters

A trade-show award is a snapshot of a much larger shift. Electric scooters are moving
from novelty to mainstream in Nepal's cities, and Ather sits at the front of that
transition.

> Winning Best Stall at NADA is a reflection of the trust riders have placed in Ather —
> and in electric mobility as the future of Nepal's roads.

The recognition reinforces Vaidya Energy's mission to make clean, smart transport the
default choice rather than the exception.

## Building the ecosystem

Selling scooters is only half the story. VOITH and Vaidya Energy continue to invest in
the infrastructure that makes EV ownership effortless:

1. A widening network of charging stations across key corridors
2. Trained service technicians and accessible spare parts
3. Education and demo programmes that let riders experience EVs first-hand
